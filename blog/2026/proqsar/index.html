Announcement

🎉 We are thrilled to share our latest publication: ProQSAR: A modular and reproducible framework for small-data QSAR modeling with fit-and-use models in the Journal of Cheminformatics. Published on 22 April 2026, this work presents a modular QSAR workbench built to support reproducible, risk-aware, and deployment-ready molecular property modeling.

🔍 With ProQSAR, we integrate the full QSAR workflow into one flexible framework: from standardization, descriptor generation, and group-aware splitting to feature selection, model training, statistical comparison, conformal prediction, and applicability-domain assessment.

📈 Across representative benchmarks, ProQSAR delivered strong and competitive performance across both regression and classification tasks. These results highlight the value of a modular and reproducible QSAR framework that not only supports accurate molecular property prediction, but also improves trustworthiness through calibrated uncertainty estimates, applicability-domain assessment, and transparent workflow tracking.
