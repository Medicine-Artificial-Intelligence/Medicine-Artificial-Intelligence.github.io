Hi, my name is Nguyen Quang Huy Le. I recently graduated in Pharmacy, where I built a strong foundation in medicinal chemistry and drug-related sciences. During my studies, I became increasingly interested in how computational methods can support and accelerate drug discovery. In particular, I have worked on projects involving multi-target optimization, focusing on important biological targets such as EGFR and VEGFR.

Through these experiences, I developed strong analytical thinking and became more curious about how data-driven approaches can help us better understand molecular interactions and improve drug design strategies. I am especially interested in the field of cheminformatics, where chemistry and artificial intelligence intersect.

I hope to continue developing my expertise and contribute to advancing research at the interface of chemistry and computational science.
